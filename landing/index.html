3. Keep chatbots in step

Turn on sync and each chatbot’s own memory stays up to date. It shows you what it would write and waits for your OK, and every change can be undone.

You decide what each chatbot knows.

Choose who sees what

Share each memory with any chatbot, or keep it private. Pause one chatbot, or all of them at once.

See what chatbots already know

OmniMemory finds things your chatbots remember that you haven’t saved yet. Keep the useful ones.

Undo anything

Each chatbot has an activity log with one-click undo. Deleted memories wait 30 days before they’re gone.

Catches repeats and conflicts

Saving “I use Python 3.13” when you already have “3.11”? You’ll see it before you save.

An assistant, if you want one

Turn it on for 10 free AI actions a day, or add your own Gemini key. Tidy up by asking: “I moved to Austin.” Nothing changes until you press Apply.

Never sends a message for you

Memories go into the message box only when you press Add. If that doesn’t work, they’re copied for you to paste.

Your memories stay on your device.

OmniMemory has no account and no server. Everything is stored in your browser, and you can export or delete it whenever you like.

No account, no analytics

Nothing about you is sent to us. There is no “us” to send it to.

Only talks to the chatbot you use

Sync runs on the chatbot’s own site, with your normal sign-in. OmniMemory never sees your password.

Private means private

Private memories and anything that looks like a password are never shared, synced or sent to an AI.

Chatbots only get what you share

Chatbot pages can’t read your memories directly. They have to ask, and only get what you’ve shared with them.

Questions

Does OmniMemory ever send a message for me?

No. Memories go into the message box only when you press Add to message, and you read it before you send. If they can’t be typed in, they’re copied so you can paste them.

Where are my memories stored?

In your browser, on this device. There’s no OmniMemory account or server. Memories don’t sync between devices, so export a copy from Settings if you want a backup.

What does sync actually change?

Only Instructions for Gemini, plus OmniMemory’s own marked section in ChatGPT’s custom instructions and in Claude’s Instructions. Everything else is read and never changed. Sync is off until you turn it on, and the first time you see a preview.

Do I need the AI assistant?

No. It’s off until you turn it on in Settings. Then you get 10 free AI actions a day, or unlimited use with your own Gemini API key. Without it, saving, adding memories to chats, sharing and sync all work the same.

What happens if a chatbot changes its website?

Adding memories to a message may stop working for that site until OmniMemory is updated. Copy still works, and everything else keeps running.

Something isn’t working. What should I try?

Reload the chatbot’s page, check you’re signed in, and try sync again in a minute. The troubleshooting guide covers the Memory button, sync, Gemini errors, repeats and more.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome 120 or newer, and Chromium browsers such as Edge and Brave. A Firefox version is being prepared for Firefox Add-ons; it will be listed there rather than on the Chrome Web Store.

When will it be on the Chrome Web Store?

Soon. Until then you can install it from the source on GitHub: build it, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode and choose Load unpacked.
