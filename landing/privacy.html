Privacy policy

Your memories stay yours.

This page explains what the OmniMemory browser extension stores, where it goes, and what it never collects. It is part of the Terms of Use, which you agree to by using OmniMemory. Last updated 25 November 2026.

What OmniMemory stores

The memories you save (short pieces of text), their categories and tags, who each one is shared with, your settings, and a short history of changes so you can undo them. If you add a Gemini API key, it is stored too.

Where it is stored

Only in your browser, on your device. There is no OmniMemory account and no OmniMemory server, and nothing is copied between devices. Removing the extension erases OmniMemory’s copy, so use Settings → Export if you want a backup.

What leaves your device, and when

  • When you add memories to a chat. The memories you tick are typed into the chatbot’s message box. You read them before you press send. They are never sent for you.
  • When you turn on sync for a chatbot. OmniMemory writes the memories you share with that chatbot into the chatbot’s own settings (Instructions for Gemini, ChatGPT’s custom instructions, Claude’s Instructions) and reads what the chatbot remembers so it can show it to you. This happens on the chatbot’s own website, with your normal sign-in. Sync is off until you turn it on, and the first time you see a preview.
  • When you turn on the AI assistant. The memories it needs (never private ones, and never anything that looks like a password) are sent to Google’s Gemini API to suggest edits. If you press “Suggest memories from this chat”, the last few messages of that chat are sent too. With the free daily allowance this uses OmniMemory’s own key on Gemini’s free tier, where Google may use what is sent to improve its products. With your own key, your agreement with Google applies. The assistant is off until you turn it on.

Data handled by ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini is covered by OpenAI’s, Anthropic’s and Google’s own privacy policies.

What OmniMemory never does

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or telemetry.
  • Your data is never sold or shared with anyone else.
  • It never reads your passwords, and it refuses to save text that looks like a password, key or card number.
  • It only runs on chatgpt.com, claude.ai and gemini.google.com, plus the “Save selection” menu you use yourself on other pages.

Your choices

Every memory can be kept private, shared with only some chatbots, edited, exported or deleted. Deleted memories wait 30 days in Recently deleted, then are erased. You can pause all sharing with one switch.

Children

OmniMemory doesn’t collect personal information from anyone. Use of ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini is subject to their own age rules.
